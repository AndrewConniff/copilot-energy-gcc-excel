# Environment Checklist

Classification: Public

This demo uses a fictional Microsoft Fake Company created for demonstration purposes. Any resemblance to real organizations, people, products, services, or data is coincidental. Do not use customer confidential information in this repository.

## Before delivery

- Confirm whether Microsoft 365 Copilot is available in the tenant being shown.
- If using GCC and Copilot is unavailable, do not attempt a live Copilot demonstration.
- Confirm the demo does not require web grounding.
- Confirm the demo does not require agents or researcher-style agents.
- Confirm the demo does not require YouTube.
- Keep the presenter-provided PPTX local if it is not public-safe.

## During delivery

- Use fictional Contoso Energy Services examples only.
- Say when you are describing what the experience would look like in a Copilot-enabled commercial tenant.
- Do not claim feature availability in GCC unless it has been confirmed.
